Privacy Policy

Bright Steps Learning · last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

Bright Steps Learning does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. The app has no networking code at all and works entirely offline.

Who we are

Bright Steps Learning is an iOS app published by Ravikiran Reddy Gajula ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when a child or parent uses the app. It applies to the Bright Steps Learning app and to this website.

Information stored on your device

So that lessons feel personal and progress is remembered, the app saves a small amount of information locally on the device, using Apple's standard on-device preferences storage. This information never leaves the device.

WhatWhy
The first name or nickname entered on the welcome screenTo greet the learner and personalise the screen
Lesson progress, completed skills, XP and streaksTo unlock new skills and show progress
Coin balance and items collected from the Toy ShopTo keep earned rewards
Chosen subject, mascot and sound settingsTo remember preferences

Because this data is stored only on your device, we cannot see it, and we hold no copy of it.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect any of the following, and the app has no ability to collect them:

  • Names, email addresses or any account or login details — there are no accounts
  • Location data, including coarse or precise location
  • Contacts, calendars, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or device fingerprints
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or behavioural profiles
  • Any payment or financial information — the app has no in-app purchases

The app requests no iOS permissions. It does not access the camera, the microphone, the photo library, contacts, the calendar, notifications or the device's location.

No networking, no third parties

All lessons, questions, images and sound files are bundled inside the app. The app does not make network requests, so no information can be sent anywhere.

We use no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no crash-reporting services and no social media SDKs. No third party receives information about you or your child through this app.

Spoken instructions

Some lessons can read questions aloud using the voice that is already built into iOS. That speech is produced by the operating system on the device. Bright Steps Learning does not send any text or audio to a server, and we do not record or store any audio.

Children's privacy

Bright Steps Learning is designed for children, and we have built it so that no personal information is collected from anyone, of any age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information at all.

This means there is no personal data held by us to disclose, correct or delete on a server, and we are not able to identify any individual user of the app.

The app contains no advertising, no external links, no social features, no chat and no user-generated content. Nothing in the app can lead a child out of the app or into a purchase.

Deleting your data

You are always in control of the information on your device. To erase everything the app has stored, delete the app from the device (touch and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App). Deleting the app removes all locally stored progress immediately and permanently.

Reinstalling the app afterwards starts with an empty profile.

This website

These pages are served by GitHub Pages. We do not add cookies, analytics or tracking scripts to them. GitHub may process standard technical information such as your IP address in order to serve the page, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes would also be described in the App Store release notes.

Contact

If you have any question about this policy or about privacy in Bright Steps Learning, please email reddy.ravikiran@yahoo.co.in. We aim to reply within a few days.
