Bright Steps Learning

Maths & English practice for ages 4–10 · 100% offline

About the app

Bright Steps Learning is a calm, ad-free learning app that helps children build confidence in maths and English through short daily lessons. It covers early number work, addition and subtraction, times tables, shapes, spelling, comprehension and sentence building.

No account, no sign-up, no advertising, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Everything works without an internet connection.

Getting started

  • Open the app and tap GET STARTED.
  • Type the child's first name or nickname, then tap LET'S GO!
  • Choose Maths or English and tap a skill to begin a lesson.
  • Tap an answer, then tap CHECK to see how it went.

There is no login, password or demo account. The name you type is stored only on your own device and is used to greet the learner.

Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All lessons, sounds and images are built into the app. It works fully offline.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Nothing is uploaded anywhere and we hold no user records.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

None. Coins and toys are earned purely by completing lessons, and there is no way to spend real money in the app.

I can't hear any sound

  • Check the volume buttons on the side of the device.
  • If the ring/silent switch on the side of the iPhone is set to silent, flip it back.
  • Speech uses the voice built into iOS. If the voice sounds robotic, download an enhanced voice in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

How do I reset my child's progress?

Progress lives only on the device. Delete the app and reinstall it from the App Store to start from a clean slate. This cannot be undone.

Can I share progress between devices?

Not currently. Because nothing is stored in the cloud, progress does not sync between devices.

Which ages is the app for?

It is designed for children roughly aged 4–10, from first numbers through to early primary spelling and grammar.

I found a mistake in a lesson

Please email us with the subject, the question text and what you expected, and we will correct it in the next update.
